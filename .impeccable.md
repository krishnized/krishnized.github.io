# Impeccable

Design context for `krishnized.com` — the home hub for one devotee's creative offerings to Krishna.

## Design Context

### Users
Gaudiya Vaishnava / Krishna-conscious practitioners and seekers looking for creative devotional
inspiration — film, books, audiobooks, music tutorials, scores, aratis, fonts, and Q&A made as
offerings. They arrive curious, often quiet, sometimes in the early-morning sadhana hours. The job:
discover and reach these offerings quickly, and feel the spirit behind them (devotion, not commerce).

### Brand Personality
Reverent, lucid, unhurried. Three words: **devotional, luminous, exact.** The voice never markets —
it offers. Sanskrit diacritics (ṛṣṇ, ā, ṅ, ḥ) are rendered correctly always; getting them right is
part of the site's integrity (it literally offers a diacritics font). Emotional goal: the calm,
awake clarity of brahma-muhurta — the hours before dawn.

### Aesthetic Direction
**The night before maṅgala-ārati.** The page is painted from indigo night (`oklch(0.15 0.032 276)`)
at the top to first light at the footer, so scrolling down is the dawn arriving. Ink is lamplit cream
for English, moonlit silver for Russian; **ghee-lamp amber** is the one accent (numbers, arrows,
hovered titles), and **kumkum red** marks the single dakṣiṇā button. Layout after the ISKCON Montréal
"way in" block: the wordmark and a slow verse-rotator stand on the left; the offerings form a simple
numbered list on the right. Hovering or focusing an offering raises its picture (`img` in the data,
files in `/img`) behind the whole page in a lamplit duotone, dimmed so text keeps 4.5:1 over its
brightest spot; narrow screens show no pictures. Typefaces: **Ysabeau Office** (English) and
**Cormorant** (Russian, and the wordmark), both covering IAST and Cyrillic; not Cormorant Garamond,
whose Д/Л are Greek-shaped. **Bilingual, after culturalist.fi**: every label shows English and Russian
together, Russian beside it in the serif; no language toggle. Pictures must carry no lettering (a
poster's title or a video's title card fights the list).

- **Anti-references / NOT**: neon, glassmorphism, card grids, gradient text, stock "spiritual"
  clip-art, busy decoration, logos blown up as backgrounds, anything that reads as AI-generated.
- **Theme**: dark, night to dawn. `style.css` keeps the light base for the other krishnized.com
  pages that link it (license); the home page uses `home.css`.

### Design Principles
1. **Offer, don't sell.** Every word and element is an offering — concrete, sincere, no marketing.
2. **Diacritics are sacred.** Sanskrit is always transliterated correctly; type must cover IAST.
3. **Light is the soul.** Night giving way to dawn is the ground; the lamplit picture on hover is the one signature effect.
4. **Calm over clever.** Subtraction, breathing room; no scattered gimmicks.
5. **Content / presentation split.** Content lives in `_data/offerings.yml` (the offerings: [English,
   Russian] pairs, url, optional image) and `_data/verses.yml`; design lives in
   `_layouts/default.html` + `style.css`; Jekyll assembles them on GitHub Pages. To change the
   offerings you edit only `_data/offerings.yml`.
